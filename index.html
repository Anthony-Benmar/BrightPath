No stress, no surprises

How does the free first session work?+

A 45-minute session where a mentor gets to know you, finds the right starting level, and builds a personal plan. You keep the plan whether you sign up or not.

One-on-one or group, which is better?+

One-on-one gives a learner a plan and pace built around them, while group sessions add peer energy at a lower cost. A mentor can recommend a fit after the first session.

Who are the mentors?+

Mentors are vetted educators with subject expertise and teaching experience. They go through structured onboarding and receive ongoing feedback.

How do you track progress?+

After sessions, mentors log notes and milestones. Families get clear updates, and mentors review progress together with the learner.

Can we pause or cancel anytime?+

Yes. Plans are flexible, with no long-term lock-in. Contact the team to change or pause a schedule.
