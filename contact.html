How does the free first session work?+

A 45-minute session where a mentor gets to know you, finds the right starting level, and builds a personal learning plan. You keep the plan whether you sign up or not.

One-on-one or group, which is better?+

One-on-one gives a learner a pace and plan built around them, while group sessions add peer energy at a lower cost. A mentor will recommend the best fit after the free first session.

Who are the mentors?+

Mentors are vetted educators with subject expertise and at least two years of teaching experience. They receive structured onboarding and regular feedback.

How do you track progress?+

After sessions, mentors log notes and milestones. Families see a clear summary, and mentors review progress with the learner at the start of the next session.

Can we pause or cancel anytime?+

Yes. There is no lock-in and no cancellation fee. You can pause for up to 30 days or cancel anytime by contacting us before your next session.
