Good teaching listens first

How we teach

Start with the learner in front of us. Make a clear plan. Keep changing our approach until the idea lands.

01

Diagnose, don't assume

We never start from the textbook page a learner “should” be on. We find where understanding stops.

02

Explain until it clicks

Every mentor carries five ways to explain the same idea. If the first doesn't land, we switch approaches.

03

Practice, then prove it

Small wins weekly, a written report monthly. Learners see their own progress—that's where motivation comes from.
